Crate

A small personal tool for planning DJ sets from a vinyl collection.

  • Imports a record collection from Discogs, with every track
  • Adds BPM, energy and descriptive tags to each track
  • Builds playlists and smart playlists, then lists the records to pull for the night
  • Prints a sticker for each record sleeve with tracks, BPM and energy

BPM data provided by GetSongBPM. Collection data from Discogs, tags from MusicBrainz and Last.fm.
